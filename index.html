What We Do

Electrical Services Across Ballarat

From a flickering downlight to a full new home fit-out, we cover the lot — wired to Australian Standards and finished tidy.

01

New Build & Renovation Wiring

Full electrical fit-outs for new homes and renovations across Ballarat, wired to Australian Standards from rough-in to final fix. We work alongside your builder and flag anything in the plans that's been overlooked before it becomes a costly fix later.

02

Residential Electrical Repairs

From dodgy power points and flickering lights to tripping switchboards and safety switch issues, we diagnose the problem properly rather than guessing. Every job is left clean, tested and explained in plain English.

03

Lighting Design & Installation

LED downlights, pendants, outdoor and security lighting designed to suit how you actually use the space. We'll talk through layout and switching options so you're not stuck with lights in the wrong spot for the next twenty years.

04

Commercial & Fit-Out Work

Electrical for gyms, offices, shops and small businesses around Ballarat — including data, three-phase, lighting and power. We work to your timeline so trade is back up and running with minimal downtime.

05

Switchboard Upgrades & Safety Switches

Older Ballarat homes often run boards that aren't up to today's loads or compliance requirements. We upgrade switchboards and install RCDs so your family and property are properly protected.
